A LITTLE ABOUT US

A clean space is more than a look. It’s a feeling.

Presh Fresh Cleaning Services provides residential and commercial cleaning services in Johannesburg. We clean houses, apartments, offices, schools and industrial sites.

Our aim is simple: deliver quality, reliable and affordable cleaning services while treating every client and every space with respect. We take pride in friendly service and in helping customers feel at ease in clean, cared-for environments.

Based in Eastleigh, Edenvale, we welcome enquiries from customers looking for dependable cleaning support.
